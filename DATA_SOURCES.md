# Data Sources

## NOAA PC1902 ME70

This repository includes a small public sample file from the NOAA NCEI Water Column Sonar Data Archive.

- Dataset: ME70 Water Column Sonar Data Collected During PC1902
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.25921/tctw-am57
- Archive: NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information
- File used: `PC_2019_-D20190613-T030352.raw`
- Original S3 path: `s3://noaa-wcsd-pds/data/raw/Pisces/PC1902/ME70/PC_2019_-D20190613-T030352.raw`

Citation text from the NOAA README:

NOAA/NMFS/SEFSC Panama City Laboratory, Harbor Branch Oceanographic Institute, University of North Carolina Wilmington, College of Charleston. 2023. ME70 Water Column Sonar Data Collected During PC1902. NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information. https://doi.org/10.25921/tctw-am57

## Synthetic Data

The `.npz` files under `data/synthetic/` are generated by `src/build_sonar_job_dataset.py`.

They are not field measurements. They are controlled validation data with known target truth for algorithm testing.
